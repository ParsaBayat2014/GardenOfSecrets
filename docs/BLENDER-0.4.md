# Blender 0.4 production files

Seven editable Blender 5 master files live in art-source/blender: garden, shed, green, archive, raha, sohrab and saman. Runtime models are exported to app/src/main/assets/rebuild/models/blender. The classic illustrated version remains accessible from the menu and uses a separate save key.

Reproduce architecture: node tools/blender/export-blockouts.mjs followed by Blender --background --python tools/blender/build-environments.py -- all. Reproduce weighted character meshes: Blender --background --python tools/blender/build-tailored.py. Character surfaces are lofted and swept meshes authored in Blender: continuous jaw and cheek profiles, eyelids, nose, lips, fingers, tailored clothing panels, cuffs and hair locks. These are procedural stylized models, not cinematic hand sculptures. Blink and Speak morph targets animate eyelids and simple mouth motion; mouth movement is not voice-synchronized. Architectural embellishment, tile roofs, roses, ivy and room details are authored by the Blender script. KayKit skeletal rigs and animation are credited under CC0 in rebuild/licenses; Three.js is MIT.

Verification: complete chapter browser traversal, articulated knee movement, walking, pause/resume, independent legacy save and navigation checks. Android release build and lint are required after asset changes. No physical Android device has been tested. Myket submission has not occurred. The build is a one-chapter prototype, not a guarantee of commercial quality or absence of all bugs.
